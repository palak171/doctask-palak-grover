# Task 3 (growth version) — use cases, buyers, and how a conversation would start

Same ten use cases as the engineer track's Task 3 (companies and roles
only, never named people, never contacted — per this round's rule), each
extended here with where that buyer role actually listens and what would
plausibly open the door, as the growth track's version of Task 3 asks.

| # | Use case | Who buys it (companies) | Role who'd care | Where they listen | What would open the door |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-jurisdiction employment contract maintenance | Deel, Remote.com, Papaya Global | Head of Legal Ops / Employment Counsel | HR/legal-ops Slack communities (e.g. r/humanresources-adjacent Slack groups), LinkedIn posts from EOR legal ops leads | A short, specific post about the exact failure mode ("a labor-law change updated in 40 of 100 country templates, not 100") — specificity reads as credibility, a generic pitch doesn't |
| 2 | Vendor contract contradiction audits | Flexport, Faire, Instacart | Head of Procurement / Legal Ops | Procurement/legal-ops LinkedIn groups, CLOC (Corporate Legal Operations Consortium) community | A free, ungated checklist (exactly what `growth-machine/outputs/featured/` produces) — legal ops leads download tools before they take a sales call |
| 3 | M&A data room due diligence | Houlihan Lokey, Thoma Bravo deal teams, Union Square Advisors | Diligence Associate / VP | Dealmaker-focused newsletters, M&A-specific LinkedIn circles | A worked example of a real (synthetic) data-room contradiction caught pre-signing — associates share tools that make them look thorough to a partner |
| 4 | Insurance policy wording consistency | Lemonade, Root, Hippo | Compliance/Product Counsel | InsurTech-specific Slack/Discord communities, state DOI filing conferences | A piece on the specific compliance risk of rider drift across states — a regulatory angle gets read by counsel even when a product pitch wouldn't |
| 5 | RFP/proposal drafting for GovCon | Booz Allen, Leidos, smaller GovCon shops | Proposal Manager | GovCon-specific communities (e.g. APMP chapters), LinkedIn GovCon proposal groups | A compliance-matrix template that maps directly to solicitation structure — proposal managers are template-hungry by profession |
| 6 | SOC 2/ISO policy set maintenance | Vanta, Drata, Secureframe (OEM); Ramp, Brex (direct) | Head of Compliance / GRC lead | Compliance-automation partner channels, SOC2-prep communities | A partnership conversation starts with the OEM angle itself (an editing engine behind their product), not a checklist — different door than the others |
| 7 | CRE lease abstraction | WeWork, Industrious, JLL property management | Lease Administration Lead | CRE-specific trade publications, lease-admin LinkedIn groups | A "what changed and why" audit trail example — lease admins currently track this in spreadsheets and feel that pain acutely |
| 8 | Loan agreement vs. underwriting policy consistency | SoFi, Upstart, Affirm | Lending Compliance Lead | Fintech compliance communities, lending-ops LinkedIn | A piece framed as "policy vs. document audit," which is literally how this role already thinks about their job |
| 9 | Clinical trial protocol amendment tracking | IQVIA, Medpace, ICON plc | Clinical Regulatory Affairs Lead | Clinical-ops trade conferences, regulatory affairs LinkedIn groups | A regulatory-submission angle: documented change history is a requirement they already have to satisfy, not a nice-to-have |
| 10 | Investor/board document consistency | Carta, Pulley (OEM); Series C-D startups (direct) | Head of Finance/Legal Ops | Startup finance-ops communities, Carta's own ecosystem | Same OEM-first door as #6: Carta already owns this relationship, so the opening move is a partnership conversation, not content |

**Two patterns worth naming honestly:** #6 and #10 are OEM/partnership
opportunities where "content that gets found" is the wrong door — the
right door is a direct partnership conversation, which this round's rules
don't let me start. The other eight are genuine content/search
opportunities, which is why the growth machine (see
[`growth-machine/`](growth-machine/)) targets use case #2's exact buyer:
it's the same audience as the assigned engineering build, so the whole
submission tells one coherent story instead of two unrelated ones.
